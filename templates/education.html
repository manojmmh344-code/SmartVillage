HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Are scholarship dates and eligibility listed here?

No. Scheme eligibility and application dates can change. Use the National Scholarship Portal and the relevant official scheme notice to check the latest details.

Are these courses or learning platforms managed by this dashboard?

No. This page links to external education providers. Each provider manages its own content, enrollment, accounts, and availability.

Where should I check examination schedules and results?

Check the official website of your examination board or authority. The Exams and Results page links to CBSE and NTA portals; other boards publish information on their own websites.

Can I use these resources on a mobile phone?

Many linked platforms provide mobile-friendly services, but requirements vary. Check the provider's website and your internet or device requirements before using a course.
